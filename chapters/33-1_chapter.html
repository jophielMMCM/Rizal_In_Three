THE ARREST

Rizal was arrested in 1892 after returning to the Philippines from Hong Kong and was accused by the Spanish government of being involved in the revolutionary movement. He had founded La Liga Filipina, an organization that promoted peaceful reform and cooperation among Filipinos. Although Rizal did not participate in the direct leadership of the revolution, Spanish authorities viewed his writings and reform activities as a threat to colonial rule. He was arrested shortly after the founding of La Liga Filipina and was later ordered into exile in Dapitan. His arrest demonstrated the Spanish government's growing suspicion toward Filipino reformists and intellectuals. It also showed how colonial authorities attempted to suppress ideas and organizations that could encourage Filipinos to seek political and social change.
