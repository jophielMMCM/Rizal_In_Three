INTRODUCTION

José Protacio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda was born on June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna. He gained historical recognition through his works, education, medicine, and participation in reform movements. Rizal lived through the period of Spanish colonial rule that resulted in social inequality, political restrictions, and discrimination.


His development as a national hero was determined by several factors. His upbringing and education in the family played an important role. He gained intellectual ability through the Jesuits' education. Moreover, the acquisition of medical knowledge and his experiences in Europe further contributed to Rizal's formation. In addition, his literary works and speeches reflected his progressive views, as well as his thoughts regarding the colonial rule.


This blog aims to explore Rizal's biography from the perspectives of upbringing and education, intellectual awakening and literature, as well as participation in the political movement, exile, and martyrdom. Demonstrating the factors that influenced the development of his national consciousness.
