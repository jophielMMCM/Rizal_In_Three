EXILE IN DAPITAN

Rizal spent four years in exile in Dapitan, Mindanao, from 1892 to 1896. During this time, he practiced medicine, treated patients, taught young students, and worked on agriculture and community projects (Department of Science and Technology [DOST], 2022; Mayo, 2001). He established a small school where he taught science, mathematics, languages, and physical education. He also conducted scientific research, collected plant and animal specimens, and developed a waterworks system for the community (DOST, 2022).

Despite being separated from the political center of Manila, Rizal remained active in intellectual and civic work. He continued to serve the Filipino people through education, medicine, science, and community development (Mayo, 2001; DOST, 2022). His years in Dapitan showed that exile did not stop him from contributing to society and using his knowledge and skills for the benefit of the local community (DOST, 2022).
