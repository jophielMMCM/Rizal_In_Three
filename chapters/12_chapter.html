THE FIRST POEM

Rizal was exposed to poetry from a young age. The most famous poem traditionally attributed to Rizal is “Sa Aking Mga Kabata”, which is considered his first literary work. The poem refers to the Filipino language and its recognition as a fundamental layer of Filipino culture.

The authorship of the poem is questionable, as there are no credible historical sources that prove the connection between Rizal and the creation of this work. Furthermore, the first published version of the poem dates back to a later period, and the connection to Rizal's early years cannot be confirmed. Therefore, it is necessary to refer to this work as traditionally attributed to Rizal.
