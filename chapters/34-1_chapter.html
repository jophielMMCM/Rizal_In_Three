THE TRIAL

During the trial, Rizal was represented by Lieutenant Luis Taviel de Andrade, who presented his defense before the military court. Rizal also prepared his own defense and denied that he had encouraged or participated in the armed revolution. Despite his defense, the court found him guilty and sentenced him to death (NHCP, 2000).

The trial took place at the Cuartel de España in Manila and ended with Rizal's death sentence. He remained imprisoned at Fort Santiago until December 29, when the sentence was formally read to him. The following day, December 30, 1896, Rizal was executed at Bagumbayan (NHCP, 1959; NHCP, 1998).
