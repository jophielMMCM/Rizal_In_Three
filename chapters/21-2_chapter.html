DEPARTURE

While in Spain, he became familiar with the ideas and lives of other Filipino students. Rizal also spent time in various European countries, where he continued his education and became more involved in politics (Schumacher, 1977). These experiences shaped Rizal's progressive views and understanding of colonial oppression.

Finally, while in Germany, he became familiar with the fundamentals of ophthalmology (Guerrero, 1998). One of the reasons for studying medicine was the deterioration of his mother's eyesight. Rizal's medical knowledge and skills were relevant to his later activities, as he sought to help people in Dapitan.
