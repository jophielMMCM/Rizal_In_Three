THE FIRST DAY OF SCHOOL

Rizal's early years were spent at home, where his mother gave him fundamental lessons. After that, he was tutored by Celestino, Lucas Padua, and Leon Monroy. His education continued in Biñan, where he studied under the guidance of Justiniano Cruz (Guerrero, 1998)

Rizal's years in Biñan influenced his personality through the acquisition of fundamental knowledge. Moreover, the study in Biñan prepared him for further studies in Manila, where he would be exposed to a more developed educational system.
