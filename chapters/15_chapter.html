RIZAL'S ANATOMY

In addition to liberal arts, Rizal received a medical education at the University of Santo Tomas. He later continued his medical studies in Spain at the Universidad Central de Madrid. Finally, while in Germany, he became familiar with the fundamentals of ophthalmology (Guerrero, 1998).

One of the reasons for studying medicine was the deterioration of his mother's eyesight. Rizal's medical knowledge and skills were relevant to his later activities, as he sought to help people in Dapitan.
