THE STORY OF THE MOTH

One of the most notable stories from Rizal's childhood is associated with the moth. His mother told him a story about a moth that was attracted to the light of the lantern and perished due to the flame. This story later influenced his philosophical views, as well as his development as a person with a national consciousness (Guerrero, 1998).

The story of the moth and the lantern was valuable to Rizal, as it symbolized struggles, sacrifices, and the ultimate price of achieving one's goals and dreams. It can also be noted that the story demonstrated the strength of character, as well as the importance of pursuing one's beliefs and principles. Furthermore, the story indicated the association of Rizal's early years with his mother's teachings and attitude.
