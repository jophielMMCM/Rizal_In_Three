BACKGROUND

Rizal was born in a large family of 12 children. His parents were Francisco and Teodora, and his siblings were Saturnina, Paciano, Narcisa, Olimpia, Lucia, Maria, Concepcion, Josefa, Trinidad, and Soledad. His older brother Paciano later influenced Rizal's political life.

Rizal's mother's family members were also well-educated people. His maternal grandparents were Lorenzo Alonso and Brijida de Quintos. His family background and rich upbringing influenced his intellectual development and early understanding of such concepts as education, literature, religion, and social justice.
