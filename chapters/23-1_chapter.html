NOLI ME TÁNGERE

Rizal's novel Noli Me Tangere was published in 1887 and was an openly critical work that demonstrated the problems of Philippine society under Spanish rule. It depicted colonial exploitation, oppression, and injustice, as well as inequality and imbalances in local society.

Noli Me Tangere was significant in that it demonstrated Rizal's progressive views and criticism of the colonial regime. In contrast to purely ideological works, such as poems and essays, Rizal's novel used fictional characters and events to demonstrate the realities of Philippine society (Guerrero, 1998; Schumacher, 1997).
