LA LIGA FILIPINA

Rizal returned to Manila, where he established La Liga Filipina in 1892. Its primary mission was to ensure mutual assistance and cooperation between all Filipinos. The objectives of La Liga Filipina included the protection of each member from oppression, promotion of education, and improvement of the general welfare. It represented a new stage in Rizal's activities, which continued to develop despite his exile (Schumacher, 1997).
